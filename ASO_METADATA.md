### Description
BeforeAfter is the cleanest way to create interactive before and after photo comparisons. Pick two photos, slide between them, and share your transformation with one tap.

No watermarks. No subscriptions for basic use. No nonsense.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Select your "before" photo from your gallery
2. Select your "after" photo
3. Drag the slider to reveal the transformation
4. Tap share to post anywhere

PERFECT FOR:
• Fitness progress — track muscle gain, weight loss, body recomposition
• Skincare routines — document acne treatment, anti-aging, product results
• Home renovation — room makeovers, landscaping, DIY projects
• Hair growth — document treatments, cuts, color changes
• Makeup looks — showcase transformations, tutorials
• Baby milestones — monthly growth photos
• Pet growth — puppy to adult comparisons
• Injury recovery — physical therapy progress

WHY BEFOREAFTER WINS:
• Zero watermarks — your photos stay clean
• 100% offline — no upload, no cloud, no account
